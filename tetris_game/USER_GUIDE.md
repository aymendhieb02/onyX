# Tetris Game - User Guide

Welcome to the Tetris Game! This guide will help you understand how to play and enjoy all the features.

## Getting Started

### First Launch

1. **Splash Screen**: When you first open the app, you'll see an animated splash screen
2. **Choose Theme**: Select your favorite theme from 5 beautiful options:
   - 🚀 Space - Dark blue space theme
   - 🌊 Ocean - Beautiful ocean blues
   - 🌲 Forest - Green nature theme
   - 💜 Neon - Vibrant purple neon
   - 🌈 Rainbow - Colorful rainbow theme

3. **Select Mode**: Choose between:
   - **Adventure Mode**: Play through 20 levels with unique challenges
   - **Classic Mode**: Play infinitely and try to beat your high score

## How to Play

### Basic Controls

The game uses simple gesture controls - no buttons needed!

- **Move Left**: Swipe your finger left on the screen
- **Move Right**: Swipe your finger right on the screen
- **Rotate**: Tap anywhere on the screen to rotate the piece
- **Drop Faster**: Swipe down to make the piece fall faster
- **Instant Drop**: Swipe down quickly and hold for instant drop

### Game Objective

Your goal is to:
1. Move and rotate falling pieces (Tetrominoes) to create complete horizontal lines
2. When a line is complete, it disappears and you earn points
3. Clear as many lines as possible before the blocks reach the top!

### Scoring System

- **Single Line**: 100 points × current level
- **Double Lines**: 300 points × current level
- **Triple Lines**: 500 points × current level
- **Tetris (4 lines)**: 800 points × current level 🎉
- **Hard Drop Bonus**: 5 points per block dropped

### Leveling Up

- Every 10 lines cleared = Level Up! ⬆️
- Higher levels = faster falling speed
- Higher levels = more points per line cleared

## Game Modes

### Adventure Mode

Play through 20 exciting levels:

1. **Level Selection**: Choose from unlocked levels
2. **Level Goals**: Each level has a specific goal:
   - Clear X lines
   - Clear X lines within a time limit
   - Reach a target score

3. **Progression**: Complete a level to unlock the next one
4. **Challenges**: Each level gets progressively harder!

**Tips for Adventure Mode**:
- Focus on completing the goal first
- Time challenges require quick thinking
- Don't worry about high scores - just complete the level!

### Classic Mode

Endless fun with progressive difficulty:

1. **Infinite Play**: Play as long as you can
2. **High Score**: Try to beat your personal best
3. **Progressive Difficulty**: Game gets faster as you level up
4. **No Pressure**: No time limits or specific goals

**Tips for Classic Mode**:
- Focus on clearing multiple lines at once (Tetris!)
- Plan ahead using the "Next Piece" preview
- Take your time in early levels
- Build up your score before it gets too fast

## Power-Ups

During gameplay, you may encounter power-ups:

- **Slow Motion** ⏱️: Temporarily slows down falling blocks
- **Clear Line** ✨: Removes a random completed line
- **Ghost Preview** 👻: Shows where your piece will land
- **Bomb** 💣: Clears blocks in a radius
- **Freeze** ❄️: Temporarily stops blocks from falling

## Tips & Strategies

### For Beginners

1. **Start Slow**: Take your time in early levels
2. **Watch the Next Piece**: Plan where to place current piece based on next one
3. **Clear Lines**: Focus on completing horizontal lines
4. **Practice Rotation**: Learn how each piece rotates
5. **Don't Panic**: When it gets fast, stay calm and think

### Advanced Tips

1. **Tetris Strategy**: Try to clear 4 lines at once for maximum points
2. **Stack Management**: Keep your stack relatively flat
3. **Save the I-Piece**: Use the long I-piece for Tetris clears
4. **Edge Placement**: Use edges and walls to your advantage
5. **Quick Decisions**: As speed increases, make faster decisions

## Understanding the Game Screen

### Top HUD (Heads-Up Display)

- **Score**: Your current total score
- **Level**: Current level (increases every 10 lines)
- **Lines**: Total lines cleared

### Game Board

- **10x20 Grid**: The main playing area
- **Falling Piece**: The current piece you're controlling
- **Placed Blocks**: Blocks that have already landed
- **Grid Lines**: Help you align pieces

### Bottom Section

- **Next Piece**: Preview of the next piece coming
- **Pause Button**: Top right corner to pause/resume

## Motivational Messages

Throughout the game, you'll see encouraging messages:
- "Awesome! 🎉" when you place pieces well
- "TETRIS! You're a legend! 🏆" when you clear 4 lines
- "Level up! 🎉" when you advance levels
- And many more!

## Game Over

When blocks reach the top, the game ends. You'll see:
- Your final score
- Level reached
- Lines cleared
- "New High Score!" if you beat your record
- "Level Completed!" in Adventure Mode if you finished the level

Options:
- **Play Again**: Start a new game immediately
- **Main Menu**: Return to mode selection

## Troubleshooting

### Game Too Fast?
- Practice in lower levels first
- Use pause button to take breaks
- Focus on Classic Mode to practice without pressure

### Can't Rotate?
- Make sure you're tapping on the screen
- The piece needs space to rotate
- Try moving it first, then rotating

### Controls Not Responsive?
- Make sure you're swiping clearly
- Try shorter, more deliberate swipes
- Check that your screen is clean
